Rules & Terms

Important GoTradeX platform rules and information.

Important Please read before using GoTradeX.

These platform rules explain the main requirements for using your GoTradeX account. By creating an account, you agree to the terms presented during registration.

1. One Account Per Person

Each person may maintain only one GoTradeX account. Do not create or operate duplicate accounts. If you have an account problem, contact support rather than opening another account.

2. Account Security

Keep your login details secure and do not share your password or account access with other people. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account.

3. Minimum Deposit

The minimum account funding amount is $10.00 USD. Deposit requests below the platform minimum are not accepted.

4. Minimum Withdrawal

The minimum withdrawal request is $10.00 USD. Available withdrawal amounts are subject to your account balance and applicable platform rules.

5. Withdrawal Fee

A 1% withdrawal fee applies to approved withdrawal requests, according to the platform's configured fee.

6. Deposits and Withdrawals

Deposit and withdrawal requests may require administrative review before they are approved. Submitting a request does not by itself mean that a payment has been completed.

7. Trading and Account Results

Trading results can increase or decrease account value. Historical, displayed, or estimated results do not guarantee future performance.

8. Platform Status

GoTradeX currently operates with paper-trading functionality in the dashboard. The dashboard does not by itself place real-money broker orders. Any future live-trading connection will be subject to its own activation, terms, and controls.

9. Prohibited Activity

Do not use GoTradeX for unlawful activity, fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, manipulation of platform records, or attempts to bypass account or security controls.

10. Support

If you have a question about your account, deposit, withdrawal, or platform access, use the Live Chat support channel in the dashboard.

Trade Allocation Example

If total eligible capital is $200 and the admin authorises a $2 trade allocation, a user with $20 of eligible capital has a 10% share of that allocation, so the user's notional share would be $0.20. A user's profit or loss is then calculated from that user's own allocated amount.
