Help

GoTradeX Help CenterFind quick answers about the trading screen, AutoBot, Chart AI, Wallet and account controls.
Choose an asset, select a timeframe, set the amount, then use BUY or SELL. Orders are not sent to a real broker until a verified live-trading connection is configured.
Tap Assets, search for a market or pair, then select it. The selected pair appears on the main trading screen.
Tap the AMOUNT control above BUY and SELL. Use the keypad to enter the amount and press OK.
Tap the timeframe control below AMOUNT and choose the period you want, from seconds through longer timeframes.
AutoBot provides ON/OFF, paper or live mode, risk selection, maximum drawdown, Save, Reset, Start and Stop controls. Live trading requires a verified broker connection.
Chart AI analyzes loaded market candles using technical rules including EMA, RSI, MACD, momentum, Alligator and fractal signals. It is an analysis tool, not a guarantee of future results.
Chat AI is the GoTradeX conversation assistant for questions about the app, trading concepts and available controls.
Agent AI is the floating assistant. You can move it around the screen, type messages, or use microphone input where your browser supports speech recognition.
Wallet contains Deposit and Withdrawal controls. Payment processing is not connected until a verified payment provider is configured.
