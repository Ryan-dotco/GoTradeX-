Fund your GoTradeX account

GoTradeX is intended to be free to use. NOWPayments is for user cryptocurrency deposits into their own trading account—not a subscription or platform access fee.

Deposit request

Sign in to your GoTradeX account before requesting a deposit. The secure service must identify your account from your authenticated session; an email typed into this page is not proof of account ownership.

The server must confirm the available network, exact amount, fees and final crypto address at checkout. Never send funds to an address shown outside the official invoice.

What happens next?

NOWPayments creates a deposit invoice for your authenticated account.
The server verifies the signed provider notification and the expected amount/currency.
A confirmed deposit is credited once to the matching GoTradeX account.
AI/automated trading remains subject to separate user authorization and trading safeguards.
TESTING ONLY — no live deposit is available until the authenticated backend, provider credentials, webhook verification, ledger safeguards and end-to-end tests are complete.
